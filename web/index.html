Let's install Nugget on your Y1

It only takes a few minutes and we'll do the hard parts for you. All you need is your Y1 and its USB cable.

Local files

Select custom files to install instead of downloading from GitHub.

WARNING: Installing replaces the original software on your Y1. While extremely rare, it can cause the malfunction of the device. We are not responsible of the damage to your device. Only the Y1 TypeA device is supported.

Put your Y1 in Download mode

  1. Turn it off

    Make sure your Y1 is completely off and unplugged.

  2. Press the pinhole once

    Press the pinhole on the bottom of your Y1 once with a pin or paperclip. You don't need to hold it.

  3. Plug it in

    Connect the USB cable to your computer. The tool takes it from there.

Please don't unplug your Y1 while it's installing.

Turn your Y1 on and plug it back in

Nugget is installed and your Y1 has switched itself off.

  1. Unplug the cable

    Disconnect your Y1 from the computer.

  2. Turn it on

    Press and hold the center button on your Y1 and wait for it to start up.

  3. Plug it in again

    When it asks you to connect to a computer, plug the USB cable back in.

Not showing up? If you can see your Y1 below, pick it.

Adding the finishing touches

We're putting everything Nugget needs onto your Y1. This can take a few minutes.

Please don't unplug your Y1 while files are copying.

You're all set!

Safely eject your Y1, unplug it and turn it on. Welcome to Nugget.
